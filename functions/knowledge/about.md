# About Acme Cloud

Acme Cloud is a (fictional) SaaS platform that helps small teams collect, organize
and share their files in one secure workspace. It is used as the demo content for
this AI chat widget — replace these files with your own.

Acme Cloud was founded in 2020 and is fully cloud-hosted. The product focuses on
three things: simple file sharing, granular access control, and fast search across
everything a team uploads.
